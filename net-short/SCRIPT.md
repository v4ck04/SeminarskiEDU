Visual language: flat bean-head cartoon characters with ink outlines inside a colourful, toon-shaded 3D world;
a cut or a camera move every 1-3 s (push-ins, whip pans, crane-ups, aerials); depth of field on close-ups; a warm
sunset for the ending. Sound: light plucked music under the voice, small comic effects on every action.

Why it holds viewers: the question in the first second, the promise in the third, three options the viewer
instantly compares themselves to, a laugh every 10-15 s, a visible time-lapse of growth, and a last line that
names the idea so it can be repeated in the comments.

## 2. The new story (original)

Same format, different object, different world and a different idea. The reference is about **what you do with
an asset** (eat it, keep it, breed it). Ours is about **time**: selling it, saving its output, or spending it to
build a tool that works without you, paid for with a loan. None of the reference's lines, characters or shots are
reused.

**Hook formula for the series:** a question everyone asks, then "One ___ explains it."

## 5. Series plan ("One ___ explains it")

1. **One fishing net** – time vs. tools (this episode).
2. **One bucket of water** – why debt with interest drains you: two villagers borrow water, one has a leaking bucket.
3. **One seed** – compounding: one eats the seed, one plants it, one plants and keeps the seeds of the harvest.
4. **One umbrella** – insurance and emergency funds: three shopkeepers and a storm.
5. **One ladder** – leverage: borrowing to climb faster, until a rung breaks.
