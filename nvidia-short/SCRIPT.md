## Facts (sources)
- 12 Nov 2026: Huang stepped down after reports about his private life, including a relationship with a woman U.S.
  officials reportedly suspected of being a Chinese spy (China denied it); investigations by the FBI, Congress and the
  company. CFO Colette Kress became interim CEO. Shares closed down 4.3 % that day:
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-nvda-jensen-huang-second-affair.html),
  [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/12/ai-titan-jensen-huang-out-as-nvidia-ceo),
  [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo/).
- 16 Nov: lowest close since 1 July 2025; Bernstein halved its price target ($400 -> $200); a petition for his return
  passed 170,000 signatures, a source said the board was not considering it:
  [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-stock-price-jensen-huang.html).
- 23 Nov: the board unanimously reinstated him as president, CEO and director, effective immediately, after "input from
  senior levels of the U.S. government"; Kress stays CFO; an independent committee's investigation continues:
  [Nvidia press release](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director),
  [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/markets/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-ceo). President Trump said he spoke to
  the board directly (CNBC, via [CNN live](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/business/live-news/jensen-huang-reinstated-as-ceo-of-nvidia)).
- 23 Nov morning: shares up more than 30 %, adding close to $1 trillion of market value; Nasdaq and S&P 500 surged:
  [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/11/23/markets/us-stocks-rebound-intl),
  [Yahoo Finance live](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-november-23-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang-141511975.html).
  Morning figures (26-33 %, $0.8-1 T) differ between sources: re-check the close before posting.
- Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993 (company history).

Wording rules for this story: every allegation is "reported" or "suspected", China's denial is said, nothing about the
private life beyond what the board and the reports said. No claim that he was fired (he stepped down).

## Script
1. **Hook** — Eleven days after its boss quit, Nvidia hired him back, and its stock jumped more than thirty percent in one morning.
   — *a giant stock chart on a trading floor shoots straight up; traders' jaws drop*
2. **Setting** — Jensen Huang co-founded Nvidia in 1993. For over thirty years, the leather jacket on stage was the company.
   — *Jensen on a keynote stage, leather jacket, a huge chip glowing behind him*
3. Then, on November twelfth, he stepped down.
   — *flash; the stage lights go out, an empty spotlight on the jacket hanging on a chair*
4. Reports said one of his relationships was with a woman American officials suspected of spying for China. China denied it.
   — *newspapers spin in; a shadowy figure in a trench coat on the front page, a red "DENIED" stamp*
5. The FBI, Congress and Nvidia's own board all started asking questions.
   — *three doors open at once: agents, senators, board members with clipboards*
6. **Action** — Without him, the stock fell to its lowest close in sixteen months, and one big bank cut its price target in half.
   — *the chart slides down; an analyst snips a "$400" sign in half with giant scissors*
7. Fans signed a petition to bring him back. More than a hundred and seventy thousand of them.
   — *a scroll of signatures unrolls down the HQ steps*
8. The board said it wasn't even considering it.
   — *a board member closes a door on the scroll*
9. **Snap** — Then on Monday morning, the same board voted, unanimously, to give him his job back.
   — *every hand in the boardroom goes up at once; the door bursts open, Jensen in the jacket*
10. **Deadpan double** — Luckily, the President said he had called the board himself. Unluckily, the investigation is still going.
    — *a phone rings in the boardroom; behind the window, the investigators are still taking notes*
11. **Explanation** — Why forgive so fast? Because to many investors, Nvidia isn't a chip company. It's one man's plan.
    — *a crowd of investors looking at a giant portrait of Jensen instead of the chips*
12. It's like a restaurant losing its famous chef. The kitchen is the same, but people stop booking tables.
    — *a restaurant: empty tables, then the chef walks in and the queue goes round the block*
13. **Consequence** — By lunch, Nvidia was worth close to a trillion dollars more, and the whole market jumped with it.
    — *the Nasdaq board turns green; confetti on the trading floor*
14. **Callback twist** — Eleven days, one exit, one comeback. The most valuable thing Nvidia owns might not be a chip. It's a leather jacket.
    — *golden light: the jacket back on his shoulders, the chart behind him, he walks back into HQ*
