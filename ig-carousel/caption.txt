Isaac Newton lost a fortune on stocks. A janitor named Ronald Read left almost $8 million. Same market, different habits.

In 1720 Newton sold his South Sea Company shares early, then bought back in near the top. The South Sea Bubble burst and took his profit with it.

Read pumped gas and cleaned floors in Vermont. He bought boring dividend stocks, reinvested the dividends and held for decades.
