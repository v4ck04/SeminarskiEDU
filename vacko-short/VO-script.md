## Za AI voice alat (samo nalepi)

```
Why do most people stay broke?
You get paid... and then this happens.

It's not always how much you earn.
Shopping. Food delivery. Subscriptions. Impulse buys. Credit cards.
Until there's almost nothing left.

It's how you manage your money.
Income goes up... but so does spending.
So your savings barely move.
Sound familiar?

Spend smarter. Cut what you don't really need.
Save consistently. Small amounts add up.
Track your money. Know exactly where it goes.

Build better money habits.
Vacko. Smart money. Smarter future.
Follow for more.
```

Napomene:
- Ime piši **„Vacko"**, a ne „VACKO", da ga AI glas ne bi spelovao slovo po slovo. Izgovor: *VAK-oh*.
- Najlakše je generisati red po red i svaki red staviti na njegov start iz tabele.
- Ako glas ispadne predug, ubrzaj ga 5–10 % u montaži umesto da sečeš tekst.
- „Follow for more." je opciona završna rečenica: izbaci je ako ti ne treba.
