# Story formula for 45–65 s animated story shorts
(From measuring several top story-short channels: 16–35 M views per short. Rules, not copies.)

## Hook (first 1.7–5 s)
- ONE sentence, no intro, no year, no "once upon a time".
- Built on a contradiction. Three types:
  - Strange action by "He/She": *"He paid strangers to push empty carts around his store."*
  - Paradox fact: *"X is illegal here, yet there is one on every street."*
  - Conditional promise: *"If you do X, you might Y."*
- Strange-action + underdog hooks performed best.

## Story arc
1. Someone does something that looks stupid.
2. People laugh at him — short quoted lines ("You're ruining my store!").
3. It gets worse.
4. He was right all along — the turn ("You see, …").
5. The people who laughed pay for it (karma) or are left speechless.

## Details
- Small exact numbers, not statistics: "every 12 feet", "three years", "until six o'clock".
- Grow things in threes: "One dropped. Then another. Then all eight."
- Show the result FIRST, then the reason, in 1–3 sentences with an everyday analogy.

## Ending
- Last line = twist, punchline or callback. End 0.1–0.5 s after the last word. No goodbye, no "subscribe".

## Pace & length
- 145–250 words, 3.3–3.8 words/s, very few pauses (longest ≈ 0.4 s).
- 3rd person for historical tales; 2nd person ("Imagine you…") for explainers.

## Captions
- 1–3 words at a time, word-synced, bold white with dark outline, centred at ~70 % of frame height, ~3 % of frame height.
- Extra text lives INSIDE the world: newspaper headline, a sign on a door, a label on a jar.

## Picture
- 3D toon world + flat 2D characters; simple characters, detailed hero object.
- Something moves in ~90 % of frames; scene change every 1–3.5 s (cuts or travelling camera).

## Sound
- −14 LUFS master. One narrator. A music/ambience bed that never stops, 10–15 dB under the voice; SFX tucked under the voice.
