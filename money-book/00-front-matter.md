# The Money Parables

### 12 Short Stories That Explain Money Better Than School Did

*By Vacko | Money Stories*

---

## A note before you start

This book teaches how money works. It does not tell you what to buy, sell or invest in. Everyone's situation is
different: your income, your debts, your country's tax rules, your goals. For decisions about your own money, talk to a
licensed professional.

Every fact in this book comes with a source at the end of its chapter. The stories are parables: made up on purpose, to
make one idea impossible to forget. The numbers next to them are real.

*Educational content, not financial advice.*

---

## How to use this book

Every chapter has four parts.

1. **The Story.** A two-minute parable from the river town. Three men, one river, one idea.
2. **What Really Happens.** The same idea in the real world, with real numbers.
3. **Who Wins, Who Loses.** The idea seen from every side, so you can tell which side you are on.
4. **Your Move.** One small thing you can do this week. Not ten things. One.

At the end of each chapter there is a worksheet. Fill it in with a pen. A book you write in is a book you remember.

You can read the chapters in any order. If you only read one, read the one that made you uncomfortable when you saw
its title.

---

## Meet the river town

**The first man** wants things now. He is not stupid and he is not lazy. He works hard. He just never asks what his
choices cost him later.

**The second man** is careful. He saves every week and borrows nothing. He is safe, and he thinks safe means the same
thing as smart. Sometimes it does. Sometimes it doesn't.

**The third man** asks one question before every choice: *Will this still be working for me next year?* He is not
lucky. He is patient.

**The lender** wears a top hat and never fishes. He doesn't need to.

You will probably see a little of yourself in all three men. That's the point.
