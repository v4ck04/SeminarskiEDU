# RickClips style profile

_Version 23 - updated 2026-11-22T09:35:54+00:00 - built from 8 reference video(s)._

Status meanings: **frequently observed** = consistent in at least 3 videos; **occasionally observed** = present in some videos or variable; **uncertain** = too little or too weak evidence (never used as a production rule).

## Overall editing identity

- Fast vertical edit with ~2.6s shots (19 cuts/min).
- Burned-in captions at 70% of frame height, ~1 words at a time.
- Narration around 244 words per minute.
- Most shots use zoom in movement.
